# AFTERGLOW — script & master timing sheet

A 3-minute motion-design film about nostalgia for the '90s, the 2000s and the 2010s:
why memories glow, what we actually miss, and why today will glow too.

- Format: 1920×1080, 24 fps, 182.0 s (3:02 incl. 2 s fade-out tail)
- Music: 80 BPM, 4/4, **1 bar = 3.0 s**, music bar `m0` starts at **9.0 s** → bar `m` starts at `9 + 3m`
- Key: D major (with the borrowed, bittersweet iv chord Gm6)
